# Unicode Script Sampler

A modernization of the classic Unicode 3.2 sample page: for each
script, the working alphabet (or a representative character set) and a
real sentence. Unless noted, sentences are the opening of Article 1 of
the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in that language; entries
marked "pangram" or "traditional" use a well-known native text
instead. Intended for eyeballing terminal rendering: coverage,
fallback, shaping, bidi, and width.

## Zalgo and the combining-mark lab

Not a script — the stress case that lives inside every script. All
lines are width-honest (marks are zero-width); only the ink varies by
renderer and font.

Tower (six marks above): à́̂̃̄̅
Cellar (five marks below): a̖̗̘̙̜
Both directions: ò̖̗̘́̂
Enclosing (a + U+20DD): a⃝
Wide base (日 + acute): 日́
Spanning tie (t + U+0361 + s): t͡s
Real-world depth two (Vietnamese): ế ề ể ễ ệ
Full zalgo, three cells wide no matter what: h̸̡̪̯ͨ͊̽̅̾e̮̟͈̣̖̰̩̹͈̾ͨ̑l̸̡̪̯ͨ͊̽̅̾
