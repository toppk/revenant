# Revenant

Revenant is an X11 terminal with an Xt/Athena interface and a libghostty-vt
core. It is assessed independently of both xterm and Ghostty: sharing a
protocol, resource name, or parser does not establish application support.

The records below identify development revisions where evidence was reviewed.
They do not claim the first released version containing the feature. Most
features have not yet been assessed in this registry.

- [Project and implementation](https://github.com/toppk/revenant)
- [Compatibility differences](https://toppk.github.io/revenant/docs/compatibility/drift/)
